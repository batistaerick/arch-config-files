# rose-pine Artwork

Batch 01: two original AI-generated wallpapers, installed at 3440 x 1440 PNG.

Generated with the built-in image generator, then Lanczos-upscaled with a minimal centered aspect-ratio crop. These are not native 3440 x 1440 generations.

Old wallpapers are preserved in references/originals, outside the wallpaper picker. IMPORT-LICENSE continues to apply to those reference assets. Their rights are not changed by archival.

Future batch: add two more original scenes using the references for mood/palette only, without reproducing an existing composition or franchise.
