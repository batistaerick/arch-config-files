# Witcher Artwork

Four Witcher fan-art wallpapers generated with the built-in image tool, then Lanczos-upscaled to 3440 x 1440 with a minimal centered crop. These are not native-resolution generations. No lettering or text is included in the artwork.

Default: backgrounds/01-the-long-road.png. The local theme retains its previous One Dark-derived palette and working upstream editor theme identifiers.

The four previous generated wallpapers still present at rename time and their prompt notes are retained in references/previous-generated, outside wallpaper pickers. Earlier third-party references remain in references/originals. Previously removed wallpapers are not restored.

These images depict Witcher characters and iconography. Generation does not clear franchise rights for redistribution; review before shipping a public ISO.
